Data-driven cultural research and policy

Gōsān is an independent research institute and cultural think tank devoted to the governance, economics, and law of culture in a free, secular, and democratic Iran. The name “Gōsān” refers to the Parthian minstrels who kept a nation’s memory alive through culture. We aim for Iran’s prosperous future by insisting on culture as a vital infrastructure on which a country stands.

Direction and the Academic Advisory Council

The Institute is small by design. Content decisions rest with specialists; every publication passes an independent quality gate.

The Academic Advisory Council

The Council advises the think tank on research direction and the guardianship of scholarly standards; the management of the Institute and responsibility for what is published rest with the editorial board and the Research Director. Members serve unpaid, in renewable three-year terms, and are named only with their consent.

Support the work

Gōsān accepts neither advertising nor funding from anywhere. The costs of the review and the think tank — the fees of writers and researchers, editorial pay and technical costs — are covered entirely by public donations. No supporter influences the content, and none sees a finding before publication.

The Circle of Friends of Gōsān · Freundeskreis

Enduring cultural and research institutions have always been carried by a circle of friends. Gōsān’s friends hold a special place with us, and Gōsān expresses its gratitude to them in fitting ways; the content of Gōsān remains free for all, and the friends’ support is not the purchase of a privilege for themselves, but a share in the endurance of an institution they love.

Ways to give

Giving by PayPal and international bank cards goes live with the official launch and will be available right here; the association’s IBAN will be announced after registration and account opening, and SEPA transfers are free within the euro area. After recognition by the tax office (§ 60a AO), German donation receipts (Zuwendungsbestätigung) will be issued — until then gifts are welcome but carry no receipt. Under EU law, donors elsewhere in Europe may claim relief in their own country, with documents provided on request, and for larger European gifts a local tax-receipt route via the Transnational Giving Europe network will be assessed and announced. Giving from the U.S. by card and PayPal is straightforward; a U.S. tax-deductible route via an American Friends Fund follows charitable recognition. For institutional support — foundations standing behind a working group or a programme — write to the Office of the Director.

Transparency · Transparenz

The estimated annual need of the review and the think tank is about €65,000: roughly 37% reaches writers and researchers directly as fees; about 22% is editorial and management pay; about 22% covers support, editing and translation; and only about 4% is technical cost (hosting and tools). The remainder is a precautionary reserve. This is the founding-phase budget: Gōsān has defined its fifteen-year financial plan in three phases; each phase grows only on secured recurring income, and fixed costs never run ahead of it. Gōsān publishes an aggregate annual report of its funding and spending; supporters are never named without their own wish.

The Gōsān Institute e.V. is on the eve of formal registration in Berlin. Donations can be received once the association is registered and its bank account opens; this page introduces the ways to give already.
