Support the work

The Institute is funded by donations, grants and foundation support. It takes no money that comes with a conclusion attached — the funding–findings firewall in the Charter is the condition of every gift.

Enduring cultural and research institutions have always been carried by a circle of friends. Gōsān's friends hold a special place with us, and the Institute expresses its gratitude in fitting ways; their support buys no privilege — it is a share in the endurance of an institution they care for.

The Gōsān Institute e.V. is in the process of formal registration in Berlin; donations can be received once the association is registered and its bank account is open. Donation receipts for German tax purposes will be issued once the tax office has confirmed charitable status (§ 60a AO); until then contributions are welcome but not receiptable. The estimated annual need of the review and the think tank is about €65,000: roughly 37% reaches writers and researchers directly as fees, about 22% is editorial and management pay, about 22% covers support, editing and translation, and only about 4% is technical cost. This is the founding-phase budget of a fifteen-year, three-phase financial plan; each phase begins only on secured recurring income, and fixed costs never run ahead of it. Funding is reported in aggregate each year; supporters are never named without their consent. No funder sees a finding before publication.

حامی هنر
Hāmi-ye Honar · A special fund

Direct support for artists inside Iran

An economy in collapse hits artists first and hardest: no stage, no market, no insurance, and no state that would help rather than harm. حامی هنر (Hāmi-ye Honar، “Patron of Art”) is the Institute’s dedicated fund for direct, quiet financial support to artists inside Iran in acute hardship.

In memoriam

In the memory of the brave, freedom-loving artists of Iran who gave their lives for freedom.

What it covers

Living costs in an emergency, medical needs, working materials, and small quiet grants that keep a practice alive through the worst months.

How recipients stay safe

No public lists, no named recipients, no stories traded for money. The Charter’s contributor protection (Article 3) applies to every recipient; distribution channels are designed around their safety and are never published.

How the money moves

Every designated gift is accounted for separately from the Institute’s general funds and reported in aggregate — total raised, total distributed, never who.

What we ask of donors

Patience and discretion. Support reaches people through slow, safe routes; a donor who needs a name or a photograph is asking for something this fund will never sell.

Give to حامی هنر

Designated gifts are earmarked in full for artists. Receipts for German tax purposes follow the charitable-status confirmation; sources of funds are disclosed in aggregate.
